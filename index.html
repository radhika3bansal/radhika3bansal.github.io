Working Papers

Under Review

Justice at What Cost? Penalties, Rape Deterrence, and the Incentive to Murder

With Ugo Antonio Troiano

Increasing the penalty for a serious non-fatal crime can cost lives when surviving victims serve as witnesses. Using a theoretical model and difference-in-differences designs around U.S. spousal rape laws and mandatory minimum sentences for child molestation, we show that both reforms triggered increases in spousal and child homicides.

Under Review

Patriarchal Norms and Women's Work

With Shabana Mitra · Presented at 47th EBES Conference, Berlin

Can large-scale public employment unsettle patriarchal norms by expanding women's access to paid work? Using the India Human Development Survey and the phased rollout of MGNREGA within a local randomization RD design, we find that public employment programs catalyse shifts in social expectations around women's economic roles.
